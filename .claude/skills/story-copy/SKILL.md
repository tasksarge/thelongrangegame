# story-copy

Input: draft copy. Output: edited copy, or a rejection naming the rule.

## Rules
1. The 2016 text is verbatim and never edited or softened. Corrections sit beside it.
2. 2026 lines are under 20 words, present tense, mono "red pen" voice.
3. "Stats aren't everything" appears once, as the Act 2 setup, never as a conclusion.
4. Numbers carry the weight. No superlatives ("revolutionized", "destroyed") outside the 2016 text.
5. Seasons by end year ("2016" is 2015-16). Dates on every quote.
6. Credit before correction. Corrections address the work ("the axis is a sum"), never the author.
7. Real people appear as names and public facts only. No invented dialogue.
8. The page takes no position on whether the three-point era is good or bad. It reports the backlash.

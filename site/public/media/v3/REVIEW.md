# Media review log, set v3 (design pass 3, retro-futurist collage, 7 Nov 2026)

Chapter plates generated with GPT Image 2.6 (quality xhigh, 4K) via Higgsfield, four variants per plate, one round
each. Poster plates end with the DESIGN.md POSTER PLATE style string, Vox plates with the VOX PLATE string, plus the
research-note motifs (3 to 5 sparkles, one thin inner frame, dense-to-sparse halftone field; rough yellow circle and
highlighter stroke on Vox plates; no chrome, neon or bevels). The halftone cutouts were asked for as "photograph
converted to a coarse black-and-white halftone dot screen, like a 1970s newspaper print".

Sources (PNG, 3840 px long edge, every variant plus the winner copied to `<plate>.png`) live in `site/media-src/v3/`;
job IDs for every variant are in `site/media-src/v3/jobs.tsv`. Shipped renditions are WebP at 2400, 1600 and 800 px
(q 84 / 82 / 80) in this folder. The 9:16 source is 2160 px wide, so `hero-tall-2400.webp` is its native 2160 x 3840.

The earlier flat-vector set (68 images) was discarded when the style changed; its files and log are kept, not shipped,
in `site/media-src/v3/flat-discarded/`.

Checks per the hard asset rules: no identifiable faces or real players (statue busts allowed, human faces not); no team
or league logos, wordmarks or jersey numbers; no team colour pairings; no text or digits; palette limited to paper, ink,
blue (posters) or olive, yellow, chalk (Vox).

## Rounds and rejections

Every plate passed in round one; no regenerations were needed. Variants not chosen were rejected only on composition:
v3 of paper carried pencil squiggles on the page that could be mistaken for handwriting, and v3 of epilogue framed the
whole image instead of leaving the right third quiet.
